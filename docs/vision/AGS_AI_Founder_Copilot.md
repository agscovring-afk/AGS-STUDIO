# AGS AI Founder Copilot

## Concept

AGS AI Founder Copilot is a multilingual AI communication agent designed to assist the founder during international meetings, interviews, and business presentations.

## Purpose

Help the founder communicate efficiently in:

- Arabic
- French
- English

while preserving the founder's own knowledge, vision, and project strategy.
